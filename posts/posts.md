## LinkedIn

My TV had become the slowest device in the house.

A 32" TCL. Laggy menus, ads on the home screen, apps I never installed.

So last night I tried something different. I opened Claude Code on my laptop, typed what I wanted in plain English, and let it work on the TV over Wi-Fi.

One evening later:
→ 22 apps I never use: switched off (not deleted)
→ Free memory: 611 MB → 816 MB
→ The ad-filled home screen is gone. Now it's just Netflix, YouTube, Hotstar and HDMI
→ One press on my Fire TV remote turns the TV on, straight into Fire TV
→ Colours that looked like a cartoon: fixed

The part that surprised me: it asked before every single change and kept an undo list.

And when the new home screen had a bug on my TV, it found the cause, fixed the code and rebuilt it on my PC.

No new TV. No money spent. Just one evening.
